SYSTEM THESIS

Institutions do not lack data.
They lack resolved evidence.

Critical records arrive with incompatible identifiers, uneven quality, broken context and different access rules. Function Media’s shared engineering pattern preserves source provenance while resolving the real people, organizations, assets, places and events those records describe.
