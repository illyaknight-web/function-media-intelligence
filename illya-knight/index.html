Founder · Systems Direction

Illya Knight

Founder and Managing Member of Function Media LLC, directing the development of evidence-aware systems for complex institutional operations.

Operations before abstraction.

Illya Knight founded Function Media LLC around a practical systems question: how can institutions connect fragmented records without losing source context, professional authority, or the human reasoning behind a consequential decision?

His background spans operations leadership, regulatory compliance, organizational coordination and institution-facing problem solving. That experience informs Function Media’s emphasis on evidence provenance, resolved identities, reviewable relationships, role-based access and durable decision history.

Knight directs product architecture and development strategy across Function Media’s platform portfolio. Public materials describe research, engineering direction and technology under active development. They do not claim government approval, certification, deployment or customer adoption.
