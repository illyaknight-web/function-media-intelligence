Operations before abstraction.

Illya Knight founded Function Media LLC around a practical systems question: how can institutions connect fragmented records without losing source context, professional authority, or the human reasoning behind a consequential decision?

His work is informed by operations leadership, regulatory compliance, organizational coordination and institution-facing problem solving. That perspective shapes Function Media’s emphasis on evidence provenance, resolved identities, reviewable relationships, role-based access and durable decision history.

“The system should help people see more — not pretend to know more than the evidence allows.”

Knight directs product architecture and development strategy across Function Media’s platform portfolio. The company’s public materials describe research, engineering direction and technology under active development. They do not claim government approval, certification, deployment or customer adoption where those claims have not been independently established.
